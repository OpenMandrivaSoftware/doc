# Chat with OpenMandriva

Chat is often the fastest way to get help or talk with the OpenMandriva community. Anyone can join, and newcomers are welcome.

## Where to find us on Matrix

OpenMandriva rooms live in the official Matrix space, which you open in a Matrix client such as [Element](https://app.element.io/#/room/#openmandriva:matrix.org): [Open the Matrix space](https://matrix.to/#/#openmandriva-space:matrix.org).

- **OpenMandriva** (`#openmandriva:matrix.org`) — the main channel for getting help, asking questions, and chatting with other users.
- **Chat** (`#openmandriva-chat:matrix.org`) — off-topic conversation and general discussion.
- **Cooker** (`#openmandriva-cooker:matrix.org`) — work and technical talk with developers. This is where developers work, so mind your manners.

## Getting started

Set up a Matrix account before joining, then add yourself to one of the channels above. If you need a hand, message the `#openmandriva` channel.

## Before you ask something

- **Be patient.** People are in different time zones, and the team is small, so it can take a while to get a reply.
- **Ask clearly.** Explain what you did, what you expected, and what actually happened. Include anything that might help someone help you, like the version you are using.
- **Don't spam.** Do not repeatedly message the same person for the same request.

## Before changing a channel

If something feels wrong with a channel's behavior, do not just change it. Tell the team what you changed, why you changed it, and ask for feedback.
