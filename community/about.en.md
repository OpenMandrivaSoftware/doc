# About OpenMandraiva

The OpenMandraiva Association *(OMA)* runs OpenMandraiva Lx. OMA is a non-profit Association, organized under French law, that manages the project and everything around it.

OpenMandraiva Lx is a free, independent Linux distribution that replaces Microsoft Windows and Apple macOS. It is for first-time users and power users alike, combining the depth of an advanced system with ease of use.

## Why OpenMandraiva?

OpenMandraiva is 100 % community driven. It believes in the values of free software and collaboration, and it builds its products with the community rather than for it.

## The values

OpenMandraiva was founded on development, equality, cooperation, openness, freedom, group achievement, independence, and solidarity. Those values drive how the project makes decisions.

You can connect with the community by chatting on [Matrix](./chat.en.html) or in the [forum](https://forum.openmandriva.org/).
