# Installing OpenMandriva

Installing OpenMandriva is quick. Once you have your USB stick ready, the installer guides you through the rest.

**Important:** Back up your data before you begin. The installer may shrink, reformat, or wipe hard-drive partitions.

1. Copy OpenMandriva to a USB stick with at least 8 GB of storage. See [Copying OpenMandriva to a USB Stick](./copying-to-usb.en.html).

1. Boot from the USB stick. Your computer usually boots from it automatically. If not, press the boot-menu key during startup. Common keys include F12 and ESC; F2 and F10 also work. Most computers show the correct key during startup.

1. Start the live session. Live mode runs a full OpenMandriva session from the USB stick without modifying your hard drive. Use it to test the system before installing.

1. Open the installer, **Calamares**. From the live desktop, click the *Install OpenMandriva Lx* icon.

1. Choose your language and keyboard layout on the first page, then click *Configure*.

1. Choose how to partition your hard drive. Calamares offers three options:

   - **Install alongside an existing operating system** — keep your current OS and add OpenMandriva. This suits most users.

   - **Erase disk and install OpenMandriva** — reclaim the whole drive. This removes everything else.

   - **Format a partition and install** — control which partition holds OpenMandriva.

1. Create a user account and set a password on the final page, then click *Install*.

1. When installation completes, restart your computer and remove the USB stick.
