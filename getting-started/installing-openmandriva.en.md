# Installing OpenMandriva

You download OpenMandriva, copy it to a USB stick, and boot from the stick. The installer then guides you through the remaining steps, so the process is quick.

You also need to back up your data first. Installing OpenMandriva may shrink, reformat, or wipe hard-drive partitions, so you want a copy of your files somewhere the installer cannot touch.

1. Copy OpenMandriva to a USB stick. See [Copying OpenMandriva to a USB Stick](./copying-to-usb.en.html).

1. Boot from the USB stick. Your computer usually boots from aUSB stick automatically. If it does not, press the boot-menu key when the computer starts. The common keys are F12 and ESC; F2 and F10 are alternatives. Startup usually prints the key for your computer.

1. Start the live session. Live mode boots a full OpenMandriva session from the USB stick without writing to your hard drive, so you can try the system before committing to an install.

1. Open the installer, called **Calamares**. From the live desktop, click the *Install OpenMandriva Lx* icon on the desktop.

1. Choose your language and keyboard layout from the first page, then click *Configure*.

1. Choose how to use your hard drive. Calamares offers options depending on what is already on your disk:

   - **Install alongside an existing operating system** to keep your current OS and add OpenMandriva. This suits most users.

   - **Erase disk and install OpenMandriva** to reclaim the whole drive. This removes everything else on the disk.

   - **Format a partition and install** to control which partition holds OpenMandriva.

1. Create a user account and set a password on the final page, then click *Install*.

1. Restart your computer and remove the USB stick once the installer finishes.

{: .important }

Your USB stick must have at least 8 GB of storage, and the installer erases everything already on it.
